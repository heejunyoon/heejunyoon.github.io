## Overview

This was my undergraduate graduation project: a **deep learning safety system for personal mobility devices** such as e-scooters.

## What the system checks

Using images from a camera on the rider's side and a camera at the roadside, the system checks:

- whether the rider wears a helmet
- whether the rider is looking forward
- whether the rider follows the rules on sidewalks and near intersections

It runs in real time on a **Jetson Nano**.

## My role

I trained the deep learning models, set up the Jetson Nano and deployed the models on it, and used knowledge distillation to make the models small enough to run fast.

## Results

- One domestic conference paper {% cite 2022IEIE %} and one international conference paper {% cite 2023UR %}
- The thesis won the **Grand Prize (1st place)** for Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis at the IEIE Autumn Annual Conference 2022.
